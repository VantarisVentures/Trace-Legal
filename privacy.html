Trace Memory — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 5, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how Vantaris Ventures ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Trace Memory mobile application ("App"). We built Trace Memory to be a private, personal space for your memories — this policy is meant to be a plain explanation of what happens with your data, not just legal boilerplate.

1. Information We Collect

a) Memories and Content You Provide

When you use Trace Memory, you may choose to save:

  • Text entries
  • Dates, locations (typed by you), and seasonal tags
  • Custom tags and categories
  • A display name
  • Photos, videos, and audio recordings
  • A profile photo

The text of your memories (entries, dates, typed locations, tags), your display name, and the content you save in the App's AI library are stored in our backend (Supabase) so they can be backed up and restored. This content is yours.

b) Photos, Videos, Audio, and Your Photo Library

Your photos, videos, audio recordings, and profile photo stay on your device. We do not upload them to our servers, and they are not part of our backup. This means that if you lose your phone, or delete the App, a recovery code will bring back your memory text but cannot bring back files that exist only inside the App.

To help reconnect media after you reinstall the App, Trace Memory works with your iPhone's photo library:

  • When you choose a photo or video from your library, the App keeps a reference ID that points to the original in your library. It does not upload the photo or video. After a reinstall and recovery-code restore, the App can use that reference to copy the original back into the App, if you allow access to your photos and the original is still in your library.
  • When you take a photo or video with the App's camera, the App can also save a copy to your photo library. This is controlled by the "Save camera shots to Photos" setting, which you can turn off at any time.

We ask for photo library, camera, and microphone access only to provide these features. Audio recordings exist only on your device and are not recoverable if the App is deleted.

c) Device Identifier and Recovery Code

Trace Memory generates a unique identifier stored on your device. It is used to associate your data with your device in our backend so your memories can be retrieved and synced. It is not an advertising identifier and is not used for tracking across other apps or websites.

The App also generates a recovery code. If you reinstall the App or switch phones, entering the code restores your backed-up memories to the new installation. Anyone who has your recovery code can restore your backed-up memories, so please keep it private and do not share it.

d) Subscription and Purchase Information

If you subscribe to Trace Memory Pro, purchase and subscription status information (such as subscription tier and renewal status) is processed by Apple and RevenueCat, our subscription management provider. We use RevenueCat's anonymous identifiers and do not receive or store your payment card details — these are handled entirely by Apple.

e) App Activity Data

To make features work, we store limited activity information alongside your backup, such as badges and milestones you have earned or seen, timestamps of when you used AI features (so usage limits can be applied), and your theme and display preferences.

The App does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools. If you have chosen to share iOS analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports.

f) Problem Reports (Optional)

If you tap "Report a Problem" in Settings, the App opens an email addressed to us that you can review before sending. It contains your App version, your iPhone software version, the date, details of any recent errors the App recorded on your device (such as an error message and technical trace), and anything you choose to type. It does not include your memories, photos, audio recordings, or recovery code. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself, and we use it only to find and fix problems. The App keeps its recent error details in a small log on your device and never uploads it automatically.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App
  • Back up the text of your memories and restore it when you enter your recovery code
  • Power optional AI-generated content features (journals, timelines, biographies, themes, letters) when you allow them
  • Process subscription purchases and manage entitlements
  • Apply usage limits and diagnose technical issues
  • Communicate with you about the App, if you contact us

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your memories, photos, or recordings for advertising purposes.

3. AI Features and Third-Party Processing

Trace Memory's optional AI features (journal, timeline, biography, key themes, and letter to your future self) use a third-party AI service. We currently use Groq.

What is sent: when you generate something with AI, the App sends your display name and the text, dates, seasons, and typed locations of your memories to our server, which passes the request to Groq to generate the result. Photos, videos, audio recordings, and tags are never sent.

Your permission: the first time you use an AI feature, the App asks for your permission and explains this. If you choose "Not Now," nothing is sent. You can turn AI sharing on or off at any time in Settings → AI features.

How it is used: your memories are sent only to generate the content you requested. We do not use your memories to train AI models. Groq processes the request under its own terms and privacy practices; please review them if you have specific concerns.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We rely on the following providers to operate Trace Memory:

  • Supabase — backend data storage, sync, and the server function that relays AI requests
  • Groq — powers optional AI content generation features (only when you allow it)
  • RevenueCat — subscription and entitlement management
  • Apple (App Store, TestFlight) — app distribution and payment processing

Each of these providers processes data only as necessary to provide their respective service to us, and is bound by their own privacy and security practices.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored both locally on your device and (for the text content described above) in our Supabase backend. Data travels between the App and our backend over encrypted connections, and access to backed-up data is tied to your device identifier and recovery code. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your backed-up data for as long as you use the App. You can delete individual memories at any time within the App, which removes them from your device and, once synced, from our backend. You can also delete saved AI library entries within the App.

Uninstalling the App does not automatically delete your backed-up data from our backend, because this is what allows you to restore your memories with your recovery code. If you would like your backed-up data permanently deleted, please contact us at the email below and we will process your request within a reasonable time.

7. Children's Privacy

Trace Memory is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

9. International Users

Trace Memory is currently offered primarily to users in the United States. If you access the App from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide notice through the App.
