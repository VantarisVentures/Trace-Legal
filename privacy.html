Trace Memory — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 19, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how Vantaris Ventures ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Trace Memory mobile application ("App"). We built Trace Memory to be a private, personal space for your memories — this policy is meant to be a plain explanation of what happens with your data, not just legal boilerplate.

1. Information We Collect

a) Content You Provide

When you use Trace Memory, you may choose to save:

  • Text entries
  • Photos and videos
  • Audio recordings
  • Dates, locations, and seasonal tags
  • Custom tags and categories
  • A display name and profile photo

This content is yours. We store it so it can sync across app sessions and be backed up, and so the App's AI features can work when you choose to use them.

b) Device Identifier

Trace Memory generates a unique device identifier stored locally on your device. This identifier is used to associate your data with your device in our backend (Supabase) so your memories can be reliably retrieved and synced. It is not a persistent advertising identifier and is not used for tracking across other apps or websites.

c) Subscription and Purchase Information

If you subscribe to Trace Memory Pro or Family, purchase and subscription status information (such as subscription tier and renewal status) is processed by Apple and RevenueCat, our subscription management provider. We do not receive or store your payment card details — these are handled entirely by Apple.

d) Usage Data

We may collect limited technical information (such as app version, device type, and crash diagnostics) to help us identify and fix bugs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App
  • Sync and back up your memories across sessions
  • Power optional AI-generated content features (journals, timelines, biographies, letters) using the memories you choose to include
  • Process subscription purchases and manage entitlements
  • Diagnose and fix technical issues
  • Communicate with you about the App, if you contact us

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your memories, photos, or recordings for advertising purposes.

3. AI-Generated Features and Third-Party Processing

When you use an AI-generated content feature, the relevant memory content you select is sent to a third-party AI language model provider for processing, solely to generate the requested content (e.g., a journal entry or letter). This content is processed to generate your result and is not used by us to train our own models. Please review the practices of our AI provider if you have specific concerns; we select providers with reasonable data-handling commitments.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We rely on the following categories of third-party providers to operate Trace Memory:

  • Supabase — backend data storage and sync
  • An AI language model provider — powers optional AI content generation features
  • RevenueCat — subscription and entitlement management
  • Apple (App Store, TestFlight) — app distribution and payment processing

Each of these providers processes data only as necessary to provide their respective service to us, and is bound by their own privacy and security practices.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored both locally on your device and in our Supabase backend, which uses row-level security so that your data is only accessible in association with your device identifier. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data for as long as you use the App. You may delete individual memories at any time within the App. If you would like your account data fully deleted from our backend, you may contact us at the email below and we will process your request within a reasonable time.

Uninstalling the App does not automatically delete your synced data from our backend, since this allows you to recover your memories if you reinstall on the same device. If you would like your synced data permanently deleted, please contact us directly.

7. Children's Privacy

Trace Memory is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

9. International Users

Trace Memory is currently offered primarily to users in the United States. If you access the App from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide notice through the App.
