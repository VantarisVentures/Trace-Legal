Common questions

  • I reinstalled the app and lost my memories. If you saved your recovery code (shown when you first set up the app, and always viewable under Settings → Recovery Code), open the app, choose "Restore With Code," and enter it to get your memories back.
  • How do I cancel my subscription? Subscriptions are managed entirely through Apple. Go to your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Trace Memory to manage or cancel.
  • How do I restore a purchase on a new device? Open Trace Memory, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases."
